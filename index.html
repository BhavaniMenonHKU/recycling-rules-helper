What this tool does NOT do

  • It does not handle hazardous-waste disposal (paint, chemicals, ordinary batteries, medicine, sharps…) — it will refuse and point you to staff.
  • It does not override building-staff instructions — on-site instructions always win.
  • It does not invent rules — anything missing from the published HKU source gets an honest "not covered", not a guess.
If hall or building staff give you different instructions on site, follow them — this page is only a reading aid for the published rules.
